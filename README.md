# Crypto Hiring Analysis - Methodology & Sources

**The data behind our post: [Twin Bear Markets: Why Laid-Off Coders Are Quietly Looking at Crypto](https://blog.rezscore.com/).**

This document lists every statistic in that post, the primary source, and the
as-of date - so anyone can check our work. Where a claim couldn't be verified to
our standard, we dropped it. We say so explicitly below.

> **Our rule:** every number in a RezScore data piece traces to a named, dated,
> public source. No fabricated statistics, no hypothetical percentages. Several
> plausible-sounding figures (a precise count of open crypto jobs, a "30% of
> Microsoft code is AI-written" quote, specific altcoin calendar-year returns)
> were considered and **cut** because they did not survive verification. They are
> listed in "Claims we dropped" at the bottom.

## Key Findings

- **Two bear markets bottomed at once.** The tech labor market is in a multi-year
 contraction *and* crypto prices are deep in a drawdown - an unusual coincidence,
 since one is normally a refuge when the other is down.
- **The labor downturn hits the young hardest.** Early-career workers (22-25) in
 AI-exposed roles fell ~16% relative to experienced peers; young software
 developers fell ~20% from their late-2022 peak.
- **Crypto's price chart says capitulation; its money flow says records.**
 Stablecoin market cap hit an all-time high ($317B, Mar 2026) and institutions
 are tokenizing real assets - even as BTC sits ~47% below its high and the
 Fear & Greed Index reads "Extreme Fear."
- **VC funding is off its peak but still billions.** ~$30B/yr at the 2021-22 peak
 → ~$16B annualized pace in 2026.
- **The premium is in specialized skills.** Crypto cryptography/security and
 Solidity/Rust engineering pay $150k-$178k average.
- **More code, fewer coders.** AI now writes a rising share of all code while the
 active crypto-developer base is roughly flat and consolidating around
 experienced contributors.

### News & topic discovery
We used [Leviathan News](https://leviathannews.xyz/) as a discovery layer to
surface fresh, relevant research (e.g. Delphi Digital's token-markets work,
a16z's tokenization analysis) and to gauge what the industry is actively
discussing - over the trailing week (as of Jun 4, 2026), the most-discussed
topics were Stablecoins, Markets, Institutional Adoption, Real-World Assets,
Infrastructure, and Payments. Quantitative figures are cited to their primary
sources above, not to the news layer.

## Methodology

1. **Two-pass fact-finding.** We ran fan-out web research across the labor and
 crypto angles, then a focused second pass on funding, prices, jobs, and
 developer counts.
2. **Adversarial verification.** Each candidate statistic faced a 3-vote
 refutation check; a claim had to survive (no 2/3 refute) to be published.
3. **Primary sources preferred.** Where a secondary outlet relayed a primary
 figure (Galaxy, Electric Capital, Pantera, alternative.me, earnings calls),
 we attribute the primary source.
4. **One tracker per metric.** Crypto VC totals diverge by tracker; we use
 Galaxy Research throughout rather than mixing house numbers.

## Limitations

- **Point-in-time.** Crypto prices, the Fear & Greed Index, and rwa.xyz balances
 are live figures and will drift. Each carries an as-of date.
- **Crowd-aggregated layoff data.** layoffs.fyi is not an official government
 statistic; other trackers run lower. Use the figures directionally.
- **Descriptive, not causal.** Stanford's young-developer decline is descriptive
 and confounded by interest rates and the 2022 R&D tax-law change - it is not a
 clean measure of AI causation.
- **Stale developer report.** The Electric Capital figures are from the 2024
 report (~18 months old by mid-2026); a newer report may show a different
 headcount.
- **Interested parties.** Pantera (altcoin figures) and a16z (tokenization) are
 crypto investors; their framing is directionally bullish and is labeled as such.
- **Aggregated salary data.** web3.career figures are scraped from listings, not a
 rigorous compensation survey - treat as directional.
- **Contested narrative.** The "CS enrollment is cooling" story is genuinely new
 and time-layered: a decade of growth that only reversed in the most recent data.

## Claims we dropped (failed verification)

- **A specific count of open crypto/web3 job listings.** A "717 → 44 monthly
 postings" figure was refuted; we publish salary-by-skill but no open-role count.
- **"30% of Microsoft's own code is AI-written" (attributed to Nadella).** Did
 not verify in our pass; we cite only the GitHub Copilot 20M-users figure.
- **Specific altcoin calendar-2025 returns presented as drawdowns** (or vice
 versa). The Pantera figures are peak-to-trough from the late-2024 peak, not
 calendar-year returns, and are framed that way.
- **A single-day BTC variant ($65,535 / ~49% / $128,198 ATH on Jun 2).** Refuted
 in favor of the Jun 3 figures.
